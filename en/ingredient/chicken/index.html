Cooking tips for chicken

  • Pat chicken skin completely dry and toss with a teaspoon of baking powder to boost crispness.
  • Cook skin-side down first, then flip — the underside gets direct airflow.
  • Use a probe thermometer: 74°C / 165°F in the thickest part is safe for poultry.
  • Rest chicken 3-5 minutes after cooking; juices redistribute.
  • Sauce after cooking, not before — sugary sauces burn at air-fryer temperatures.
