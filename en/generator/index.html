Frequently Asked Questions

How does the air fryer recipe generator work?

Pick a main ingredient and a flavour profile. The tool combines real air-fryer cooking data (temperature, time, doneness signs) with a matched flavour and builds a complete step-by-step recipe instantly — no AI, no waiting, no sign-up.

Are the cooking times accurate?

Yes — every temperature and time comes from established air-fryer cooking references. They're starting points: air-fryer models vary, so always check doneness and use a meat thermometer for proteins (poultry 74 °C, pork 63 °C, fish 63 °C, mince 71 °C).

Is it free? Do I need to sign up?

Completely free, no account, no email. It runs entirely in your browser, so you can even use it offline once the page has loaded.

How many recipes can it make?

With 45+ ingredients and 20 flavour profiles, the generator produces hundreds of valid, cookable combinations — plus a matched side dish and dipping sauce for each.

Can I trust a recipe a computer generated?

The cooking science (temperature, time) is looked up from real data, not invented, so it's reliable. The flavour pairings are hand-curated. We're transparent that recipes are auto-generated and not individually kitchen-tested — always use your own judgement.
